# PropIQ AI Pilot Demo Overview

## Positioning

PropIQ AI is an AI-assisted revenue recovery and lead discipline platform for
Indian real estate developers.

It is accepted for pilot-demo preparation. It is not yet accepted for
production deployment. Stage 5 has not started.

## Audience

This demo is designed for:

- Indian real estate developer owners
- Sales heads
- CRM heads
- Marketing heads
- Operations heads
- Potential pilot stakeholders

## Business Problem

Most developers do not lose leads only because buyers are not interested. They
lose leads because follow-ups are delayed, hot intent is missed, duplicate
inquiries are scattered, after-hours leads are ignored, and managers do not get
clear operational visibility.

PropIQ AI creates a disciplined operating layer that makes these gaps visible
and actionable.

## Operating Flow

```text
Lead intake
-> duplicate detection
-> AI-assisted signal extraction
-> deterministic score
-> SLA
-> handoff
-> task
-> site visit
-> queue
-> dashboard
-> pilot KPI report
```

## What The Demo Proves

- Leads can enter through a controlled intake workflow.
- Duplicate and after-hours signals are captured instead of being buried.
- Buyer intent is converted into a deterministic score and priority.
- SLA breaches and escalations become visible to managers.
- Managers can see team workload and operational queues.
- Executives can see assigned leads and next actions.
- Owners can review pilot health, KPI reporting, and integration readiness.
- Unsupported capabilities are clearly marked `Not available` instead of being
  overstated.

## Demo Boundary

This readiness pack supports a pilot-demo conversation only. It does not claim
that the product is production deployed, does not authorize Stage 5, and does
not include real credentials, buyer personal data, Supabase keys, database
URLs, service-role keys, or webhook secrets.
