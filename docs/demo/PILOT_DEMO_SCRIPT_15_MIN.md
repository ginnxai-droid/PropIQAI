# PropIQ AI 15-Minute Pilot Demo Script

## 0:00 - 1:00 Opening Business Problem

"Real estate sales teams often have enough leads, but not enough operating
discipline around those leads. Hot inquiries arrive after hours. Duplicates come
from different campaigns. Sales executives miss callbacks. Managers only see
the problem after the buyer has moved on.

PropIQ AI is an AI-assisted revenue recovery and lead discipline platform for
Indian real estate developers. The goal is to help a pilot team recover missed
opportunities and make daily follow-up accountability visible."

## 1:00 - 2:00 Before Vs After PropIQ

Before PropIQ:

- Leads sit across channels and spreadsheets.
- Duplicate inquiries are hard to reconcile.
- Buyer urgency is inferred manually.
- Follow-ups depend on memory and individual discipline.
- Managers cannot see SLA breaches in time.
- Owners see reports late or with unclear source data.

After PropIQ:

- Lead intake captures source, channel, campaign, buyer intent, budget, and
  timeline.
- Duplicate, after-hours, and AI-unavailable states are explicit.
- Deterministic scoring creates clear priority.
- SLA, handoff, task, site visit, and queue workflows create follow-up
  discipline.
- Dashboards and KPI reports are role-specific and based on persisted records.

## 2:00 - 3:00 Owner View

"The Owner dashboard is the business control view. It shows total leads, HOT
leads, qualified leads, duplicate and after-hours counts, SLA status, handoff
signals, visit activity, source distribution, team workload, and pilot health.

The owner does not need raw CRM rows. The owner needs to know whether the sales
operation is recovering leads and whether the numbers can be trusted."

## 3:00 - 4:00 Manager View

"The Manager view is for operating discipline. It shows team workload, overdue
handoffs, SLA due and breached, unassigned HOT leads, visit follow-up, duplicate
review, and escalations.

This answers the daily management question: where should attention go first?"

## 4:00 - 5:00 Executive View

"The Executive view is focused. It shows assigned leads, HOT actions, follow-up
tasks, pending visits, SLA countdowns, buyer intent, and next best actions.

The executive should not need to interpret a management report. They should see
the next action clearly."

## 5:00 - 6:15 Lead Intake Journey

"Now we open Lead Inbox and create or review a lead through the Stage 3 intake
pipeline. The form captures buyer name, phone, email, project, budget,
timeline, message, source, channel, campaign, received time, location, site
visit request, negotiation intent, booking intent, legal/RERA question, human
request, and buyer confusion or urgency.

The important point is that PropIQ AI captures the operating context at intake,
not later during manual cleanup."

## 6:15 - 7:00 Duplicate Detection

"PropIQ AI checks duplicate signals by phone, email, and project context. A
duplicate is not silently ignored. It is surfaced for review so the team can
avoid scattered follow-up and inconsistent buyer handling."

## 7:00 - 7:45 AI Unavailable Fallback

"AI can assist with signal extraction, but the product does not pretend AI is
available when it is not. If AI is unavailable, the UI shows `Not available`.
Validated reviewer-entered signals still drive scoring and workflow.

This is important for trust: the system should be useful without inventing AI
confidence."

## 7:45 - 8:45 Handoff And SLA

"A scored lead can trigger handoff and SLA rules. HOT leads need faster action.
Breaches and escalation levels are persisted. The system makes delayed action
visible, so managers can intervene before the lead is lost."

## 8:45 - 9:45 Site Visit Lifecycle

"Site visits move through create, confirm, reschedule, missed, completed, and
outcome states. Follow-up after a completed visit is generated once. This keeps
visit follow-up from depending only on memory or informal messaging."

## 9:45 - 10:45 Alerts And Queues

"Operational queues organize daily action: HOT leads, SLA breach, manager
escalation, duplicate review, after-hours, site visit follow-up, and AI
unavailable. The critical filter helps the team focus on the highest-risk work
first."

## 10:45 - 11:45 Pilot KPI Report

"The Pilot KPI Report gives stakeholders a shared view of source distribution,
priority distribution, SLA compliance, response delay, duplicates, after-hours
leads, visit funnel, handoff effectiveness, workload, alerts, AI coverage, AI
unavailable, and integration failures.

Revenue opportunity is explicitly `Not available`; the demo avoids fake ROI
claims."

## 11:45 - 12:30 Health And Integration Readiness

"Health status shows API, database, and configuration readiness. Integration
status clearly separates available internal intake channels from unavailable
external providers. The system does not claim successful external delivery for
providers that are not connected."

## 12:30 - 13:15 Limitations

"WhatsApp automation, telephony, AI voice calling, Meta Lead Ads, CRM sync,
payment, channel partner portal, inventory engine, production external AI
autonomy, and paid PDF export are not part of this pilot-ready scope.

They are intentionally deferred until the pilot proves which integrations
matter most."

## 13:15 - 14:15 Pilot Proposal

"The proposal is to run a controlled pilot with a small team, seeded test
accounts, and a clear success checklist. We should measure response discipline,
HOT-lead action speed, SLA visibility, duplicate review, after-hours tracking,
team workload visibility, executive next-action clarity, site-visit follow-up,
and stakeholder trust in dashboard numbers."

## 14:15 - 15:00 Feedback Questions

"For the owner: are these metrics useful and trustworthy enough for rollout?
For managers: are queues actionable or noisy? For executives: is the next
action clear? For CRM/Admin users: what fields or integrations are needed
before a live pilot?"
