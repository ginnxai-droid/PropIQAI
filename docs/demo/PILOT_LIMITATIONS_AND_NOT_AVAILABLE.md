# PropIQ AI Pilot Limitations And Not Available Items

## Status

PropIQ AI is accepted for pilot-demo preparation. It is not yet accepted for
production deployment. Stage 5 has not started.

## Not Available In The Pilot-Demo Scope

The following capabilities are intentionally marked `Not available`:

- WhatsApp automation
- Telephony
- AI voice calling
- Meta Lead Ads
- CRM synchronization
- Revenue opportunity
- Payment module
- Channel partner portal
- Inventory engine
- Production external AI autonomy
- Paid PDF export

## Why These Are Deferred

These capabilities are deferred until after pilot validation because the pilot
should first prove the operating workflow:

- Are leads captured with enough context?
- Are HOT leads acted on faster?
- Are SLA breaches visible?
- Are duplicates reviewed instead of ignored?
- Do managers trust the workload and queue views?
- Do executives understand the next action?
- Do stakeholders trust the dashboard numbers?

Once those questions are answered, the team can decide which integration or
module should be built first.

## How To Explain This In Demo

Use direct language:

"This capability is not available yet. We are intentionally validating lead
discipline, SLA visibility, duplicate review, role dashboards, and pilot KPI
trust before investing in production integrations."

Avoid implying that unavailable integrations are already connected. The demo
should build trust by clearly separating working pilot-demo behavior from future
production scope.
