# PropIQ AI 5-Minute Pilot Demo Script

## 0:00 - 0:45 Problem Statement

"Most real estate developers do not lose every lead because the buyer is not
interested. Many leads are lost because follow-up is late, hot buyer intent is
missed, duplicate inquiries are scattered, after-hours leads are not tracked,
and managers only discover gaps after the opportunity has cooled down.

PropIQ AI is designed as an AI-assisted revenue recovery and lead discipline
platform for Indian real estate developers. It gives the team one operating
layer for intake, scoring, SLA, handoff, queues, dashboards, and pilot
reporting."

## 0:45 - 1:20 Lead Intake

"We start from lead intake. A lead can be captured through the Stage 3 intake
flow with source, channel, buyer intent, budget range, timeline, site-visit
interest, and campaign context.

The point is not just to create a contact. The point is to preserve the
business context that usually gets lost between marketing, CRM, and sales."

## 1:20 - 1:55 Score And Priority

"PropIQ AI reads the structured signals and produces a deterministic score and
priority. AI can assist with signal extraction, but the score and workflow are
not left to an opaque AI decision.

The team can see why a lead is HOT, WARM, NURTURE, or COLD."

## 1:55 - 2:35 Handoff And SLA

"Once the lead is scored, the system can trigger handoff and SLA discipline.
That means the lead is not just sitting in an inbox. It has a deadline, an
owner, a follow-up path, and escalation visibility when action is delayed."

## 2:35 - 3:10 Operational Queues

"Operational queues turn hidden work into visible work. Managers can see HOT
leads, SLA breaches, duplicate review, after-hours leads, site-visit follow-up,
AI unavailable cases, and escalation queues.

This is where the sales head can ask, 'What needs attention today?'"

## 3:10 - 3:45 Role Dashboards

"The Owner dashboard shows pilot health and business-level metrics. The Manager
dashboard shows workload and escalations. The Executive dashboard focuses on
assigned leads and next actions.

Each role sees what they need to act on."

## 3:45 - 4:20 Pilot KPI Report

"The Pilot KPI Report shows persisted metrics such as source distribution,
priority distribution, SLA compliance, duplicates, after-hours leads, site
visit funnel, workload, alerts, AI coverage, and integration failures.

The system avoids fake ROI claims. If revenue opportunity is not available, it
says `Not available`."

## 4:20 - 4:45 What Is Not Available Yet

"WhatsApp automation, telephony, Meta Lead Ads, CRM sync, payment, inventory,
production external AI autonomy, and paid PDF export are intentionally marked
`Not available`. These are deferred until pilot validation confirms what should
be integrated first."

## 4:45 - 5:00 Pilot Ask

"The pilot ask is simple: run PropIQ AI with a controlled pilot team, measure
whether follow-up discipline improves, whether HOT leads are acted on faster,
whether SLA breaches become visible, and whether managers trust the operating
dashboard enough to use it daily."
