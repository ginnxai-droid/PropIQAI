# Stage 5B Pilot Demo Script

Updated: June 17, 2026

## Opening Message

"PropIQ AI is an AI-assisted revenue recovery and lead discipline platform for
Indian real estate developers.

This is a secure pilot-demo rehearsal. The product is accepted for pilot-demo
preparation only. It is not production deployed. The data shown today is fake
seeded demo data. WhatsApp, telephony, CRM sync, payments, inventory, channel
partner portal, AI voice, and production external AI autonomy are not part of
this demo."

## Role-Based Walkthrough

The demo should show three operating perspectives:

- Owner/Admin: business visibility and controlled intake.
- Manager: operating discipline and queue accountability.
- Executive: assigned work and next actions.

## Owner/Admin Journey

1. Login as Owner or Admin using seeded test credentials stored outside the
   demo documents.
2. Open the Owner dashboard.
3. Show pilot health, total leads, HOT leads, qualified leads, duplicate leads,
   after-hours leads, SLA, handoff, visit, source, and team metrics.
4. Open Pilot KPI Report.
5. Explain that the report is based on persisted demo activity.
6. Point out that revenue opportunity is `Not available`.
7. Open Integration Status.
8. Show internal intake readiness and unavailable external providers.
9. Open Health Status.
10. Explain that health/config checks show readiness without exposing secrets.
11. Open Lead Inbox as Admin.
12. Show Stage 3 intake and explain that the form captures source, channel,
    campaign, budget, timeline, buyer message, site visit interest, and intent
    signals.

## Manager Journey

1. Login as Manager.
2. Show Manager dashboard.
3. Explain workload, overdue handoffs, SLA due and breached, unassigned HOT
   leads, visit follow-up, duplicate review, and escalations.
4. Open Operational Queues.
5. Apply the critical filter.
6. Open a lead from a queue.
7. Explain how the manager can see delayed follow-up, duplicate review, and
   escalation risk without searching through raw lead lists.

## Executive Journey

1. Login as Executive.
2. Show assigned leads, HOT actions, follow-up tasks, visits, SLA countdowns,
   buyer intent, and next best actions.
3. Open an assigned lead.
4. Explain what the executive should do next.
5. Confirm that the executive view is focused on assigned work, not owner-level
   controls.

## Lead/Project Flow

"A lead enters with project context, buyer intent, source, channel, and timing.
The system preserves that context in Lead Detail so the team does not lose
important sales signals during handoff."

Show:

- lead source and channel;
- project;
- campaign;
- preferred location;
- received time;
- duplicate state;
- after-hours state;
- timeline.

## Qualification, Scoring, And Handoff Flow

"PropIQ AI can use AI-assisted extraction when available, but the workflow does
not depend on uncontrolled AI autonomy. The score, priority, SLA, handoff, and
tasks remain deterministic and explainable."

Show:

- score;
- priority;
- qualification signals;
- handoff;
- SLA deadline or breach;
- task or follow-up;
- alert or timeline event.

## Audit And Readiness Points

Call out:

- fake data only;
- role-based access;
- no secrets in Health Status;
- unavailable providers clearly labeled;
- revenue opportunity not fabricated;
- no production deployment;
- no production credentials.

## Demo-Safe Disclaimers

Use these statements during the demo:

- "This is fake pilot-demo data."
- "This is not production deployment."
- "This provider is intentionally `Not available`."
- "We are not showing real buyer data."
- "We are not using production credentials."
- "The pilot should validate workflow trust before external integrations."

## Closing Message

"The goal of this demo is to validate operating discipline: faster action on
HOT leads, visible SLA breaches, duplicate review, after-hours tracking,
manager workload visibility, executive next actions, site-visit follow-up, and
trustworthy pilot KPI reporting.

After this rehearsal, we should capture what worked, what confused users, what
is missing, and what must be fixed before any real pilot or production
readiness stage."
